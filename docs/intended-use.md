# Zeya intended use

**DRAFT for clinical review.** Approved text from `docs/review/clinical-signoff.md`, item E (27 November 2026).

Zeya is a WhatsApp education service for pregnant women in Kenya. It shares general antenatal information drawn from Kenya Ministry of Health materials, and it tells women to go to a health facility when they describe a danger sign. Some answers are written by an AI model. Zeya does not diagnose, prescribe or treat, and it does not replace antenatal care or a health worker. A licensed clinical officer reviews all of its content.

## What this means in practice

- **Danger signs.** A fixed rule recognises danger signs and sends a fixed emergency reply with verified emergency numbers. No AI model is involved on that path.
- **Common questions.** These get answer cards written in Mother and Child Health Handbook wording and approved by the clinical reviewer.
- **Other questions.** An AI model may answer, but only from the approved Ministry of Health entries. A rule-based check reviews each answer before it is sent.
- **Release 1** is used with synthetic test participants only. Use with real participants needs ethics approval, a NACOSTI licence, ODPC registration and a DPIA first.

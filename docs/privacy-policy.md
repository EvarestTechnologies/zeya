# Zeya Privacy Policy

**Operator:** Evarest Technologies Ltd, Kenya  
**Service:** Zeya, an antenatal education chatbot on WhatsApp  
**Last updated:** 17 October 2026  
**Contact:** contact@evarestke.com · +254723469406

## 1. About Zeya

Zeya provides educational information about pregnancy based on World Health Organization (WHO) and Kenya Ministry of Health guidelines.

Zeya is part of a research study. It does not diagnose medical conditions, provide emergency medical care, or replace advice from a qualified health worker. If you experience a pregnancy-related danger sign, seek medical attention immediately.

## 2. What information we collect

When you interact with Zeya, we may collect:

- Your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp identifier.
- The name you provide.
- Your stage of pregnancy and expected delivery date, if provided.
- Your consent status and the date and time consent was recorded.
- Your preferred language and study group.
- Messages you send and responses generated by Zeya.
- Relevant technical and operational records needed to run, secure and troubleshoot the service.

We do not ask for your national ID number, exact location or payment details.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Respond to questions with pregnancy education relevant to your stage of pregnancy.
- Provide appropriate guidance when a message indicates a possible pregnancy-related danger sign.
- Administer the study, monitor the service and analyse research findings.
- Maintain service security and investigate technical problems.

Messages identified as containing danger signs are handled using the study's designated safety-response process and are not sent to an AI model, in accordance with the system's configured safety workflow.

For research analysis, we use a study number instead of your name or phone number wherever possible. This is called pseudonymisation. Research records linked to a study number may still constitute personal data if they can be linked back to you.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

## 4. Who processes your information

The service involves the following providers:

- **Meta (WhatsApp):** Transmits messages between you and Zeya.
- **Open Chat Studio:** Runs Zeya and stores conversations within the infrastructure configured by Evarest Technologies Ltd.
- **OpenRouter and Google (Gemini):** May process messages and relevant conversation context to generate educational responses to questions that are not classified as danger signs.

Information sent to AI providers may include your message, relevant earlier messages and responses, your stage of pregnancy and your preferred language. Your phone number is not intentionally included in the AI prompt.

The processing, retention and deletion practices of these providers are subject to their applicable terms, configurations and agreements.

Access to research conversations is restricted to authorised members of the study team who need the information for their assigned responsibilities.

## 5. Consent and voluntary participation

Zeya asks for your consent before enrolling you in the research study or collecting information for research purposes.

Participation is voluntary. If you decline consent, Zeya will not enrol you in the study.

Zeya is open to pregnant women anywhere in Kenya, and only women enrolled through the study team take part in the research study. If you start using Zeya on your own, Zeya asks for your consent to use the service and to store your messages. Your messages are then not included in the research study.

You may withdraw from the study at any time by contacting the study team. Withdrawal stops further research participation and collection as applicable. Information already collected may be retained where required or justified by an applicable legal obligation, an approved research protocol or a documented research-integrity requirement. The study team will explain any applicable limitations on deletion.

Withdrawing from the study does not affect your access to care at a health facility.

## 6. How long we keep your information

Evarest Technologies Ltd applies a retention schedule intended to ensure that personal information is not kept longer than necessary for its stated purposes.

- **Contact details and WhatsApp identifiers:** These are retained for as long as necessary for participant communication, study administration and any documented legal or research requirements. Their retention is reviewed when these purposes end.
- **Names and consent records:** These are retained for the period necessary to document participation and meet applicable legal, ethical and research requirements.
- **Conversation histories:** These are retained for the period justified by the study protocol and operational requirements. Their continued retention is reviewed when they are no longer needed for these purposes.
- **Research records:** Pseudonymised research records may be retained for up to 10 years after the study ends, if this period is confirmed by the approved research protocol and applicable requirements.
- **Backups and technical logs:** These are subject to separate retention and deletion arrangements appropriate to their purpose.

When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised, as appropriate and subject to applicable requirements. We also assess the handling of relevant copies held by service providers.

The final retention periods will be established in accordance with the approved research protocol and applicable legal and ethical requirements.

## 7. Your data protection rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, you may request to:

- Access your personal information.
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Request deletion of your personal information.
- Object to or request restriction of certain processing where applicable.
- Withdraw consent to participate in the study.

To exercise these rights, contact contact@evarestke.com or call +254723469406.

Requests will be handled in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and applicable regulations. Where information must be retained for a documented legal or research requirement, we will explain the applicable limitation.

## 8. Data security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure.

Access is restricted according to study responsibilities. The study team reviews the safeguards used to protect participant records, research datasets and information processed by service providers.

No digital service can guarantee absolute security. Where required, personal data breaches will be handled and reported in accordance with applicable law.

# Zeya Privacy Policy

**Operator:** Evarest Technologies Ltd, Kenya
**Service:** Zeya, an antenatal education chatbot on WhatsApp for pregnant women in Migori County
**Last updated:** [DATE]
**Contact:** [CONTACT EMAIL] · [PHONE]

Zeya is part of a research study. It gives educational information about pregnancy
based on World Health Organization and Kenya Ministry of Health guidelines. It does not
diagnose illness or replace a health worker.

## What we collect

When you message Zeya we collect:

- your WhatsApp phone number and the ID that WhatsApp gives us for you
- the name you give us
- how many weeks pregnant you are, and your expected delivery date
- whether you agreed to take part in the study, and when
- your preferred language
- the study group you are in
- the messages you send and the replies Zeya sends

We do not ask for your national ID, your exact location, or payment details.

## How we use it

- To reply to your questions with information that fits your stage of pregnancy.
- To tell you to seek care straight away if your message describes a danger sign, such as
  bleeding or convulsions. These messages get a fixed reply with health facility contacts
  and are not sent to an AI model.
- For research. Researchers analyse the messages under a study number (for example
  STUDY_0042) instead of your name or phone number. Published results never identify you.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## Who processes it

- **Meta (WhatsApp)** carries the messages between you and Zeya.
- **Open Chat Studio**, hosted by Evarest Technologies Ltd, runs Zeya and stores the
  conversations.
- **OpenRouter and Google (Gemini)** generate the replies to questions that are not
  danger signs. They receive the message, the earlier questions and answers in the
  conversation, how many weeks pregnant you are and your preferred language. They do
  not receive your phone number.

Only members of the study team can see conversations.

## Consent

Zeya asks for your consent before it collects anything for the study. If you reply NO,
Zeya does not enrol you. Taking part is voluntary.

## Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to see your data, correct it, or
have it deleted, and you can withdraw from the study at any time. Contact us using the
details above. Withdrawing does not affect your care at any health facility.
